== Oct 13–20, 2026
After seeing Kimi’s No Genre event, I quickly chose a direction I find interesting, recursive self-improvement, and learned the field. Then I ran [[a study]] on whether self-improving agents’ gains are real, and built [[an open-source toolkit]] for checking them.
> a study: In a self-improvement loop on Kimi, an agent revised its own playbook to raise an LLM judge’s score. Judge scores rose while accuracy against the answer key fell by 12 points. The pattern held across three judges (Kimi K2.6, Kimi K3, DeepSeek) and two real-world tasks, business-data analysis and contract review, with predictions pre-registered.
> toolkit: realgain takes any self-improvement loop’s evaluation log and separates each claimed gain into real improvement, luck, fitting the selection tasks, a fooled judge, or the agent reading the answer key. [GitHub ↗](https://github.com/imlizhuowei/realgain)
